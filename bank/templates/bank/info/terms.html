Terms of service

The rules that apply to your Kwetu Bank account.

Opening an account

You apply online with your name and national ID. We verify your identity before opening the account; you can't transact until it's approved. Once open, you receive an account number and a debit card.

Payments

Every deposit, withdrawal and transfer is screened for fraud and approved by the bank before your balance changes. We may block a payment, and temporarily restrict your account, if we suspect fraud.

Your card

Withdrawals need an active debit card. Freeze your card immediately if it's lost or you don't recognise a payment.

Security

You're signed out after {{ idle_minutes }} minutes of inactivity, and signing in on another device ends your previous session. Never share your password.

Loans

Loans are subject to approval. Repayments are fixed monthly instalments, and you may repay early at no extra cost.
